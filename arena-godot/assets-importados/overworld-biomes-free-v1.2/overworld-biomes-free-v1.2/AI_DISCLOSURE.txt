AI DISCLOSURE
Overworld Biomes - Free Starter v1.2

The pixel art in this pack was generated with PixelLab, an AI tool for
pixel art, and then worked on by hand: tiles were selected, rejected and
regenerated against a written style guide, seams and palettes were
corrected, the Wang sheets were assembled and verified, and everything was
used in a shipped game before it was offered here.

Some buyers do not want AI-generated art, and some storefronts require the
disclosure. Both are legitimate. You should know before you download, not
after. This pack is labelled as AI-generated on every store we publish on.

Some game jams and communities have their own rules about AI-generated
content - check the rules of any place you intend to publish.
